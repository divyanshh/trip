### Why these spots
- **⭐ Sacré-Cœur** — white-domed basilica crowning Montmartre; **free entry**, and the steps give one of the best free panoramas of Paris. Go early. (Optional: climb the dome for a small fee.)
- **⭐ Montmartre village** — Paris's old artists' hill: **Place du Tertre** street painters, the tiny **Clos Montmartre vineyard**, **Rue de l'Abreuvoir** (most-photographed lane), **Le Mur des Je t'aime**.
- **⭐ Place des Vosges / Marais** — Paris's oldest planned square (1612), symmetrical arcades, Victor Hugo's corner house. The crooked medieval streets are the best aimless-wander zone in the city.
- **💎 Covered passages** — gorgeous 1800s glass-roofed arcades, mosaic floors, old bookshops — a true hidden gem.
- **💎 Canal Saint-Martin** — tree-lined canal with arched iron footbridges; locals picnic by the water. Perfect slow-down before the finale.
- **🗼 Eiffel Summit** — all the way to the **top** at night, watching Paris light up, then the **10 PM sparkle** from Champ de Mars — the ideal last-night bookend.

## 🚆 TRANSPORT — Aug 31 (Metro)
- **To Montmartre:** Metro **Line 4 → Line 12 to Abbesses** (deepest station in Paris — take the **lift**, don't climb the spiral), or **Anvers (Line 2)** then the **Montmartre funicular** up the hill. The **funicular is covered by a normal metro ticket** — use it, save the step-climb.
- **Montmartre → Le Marais:** Metro (Line 12 → Line 1) to **Saint-Paul** / **Chemin Vert**, ~20 min.
- **Marais → passages → Canal:** walkable or one metro hop; passages sit around Bourse/Grands Boulevards, the canal around République/Jacques Bonsergent.
- **To the Eiffel Tower (evening):** Metro **Line 6 → Bir-Hakeim**, or **RER C → Champ de Mars – Tour Eiffel**, or **Line 9 → Trocadéro** (postcard front-on view first). ~30–40 min from Marais/Canal.
- **Cost:** single metro **t+ ticket ~€2.15**; a carnet/Navigo Easy is cheaper per ride. Everything today is inside normal metro zones — no special ticket needed (that was only for Versailles).

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)

**Montmartre is one of the most-filmed neighbourhoods on Earth — you're walking through movie sets all day.**

### 🎥 Hollywood / French cinema
- **Amélie (2001)** — *the* definitive Montmartre film. Exact spots you'll pass:
  - **Café des Deux Moulins** (15 Rue Lepic) — the café where Amélie waitresses. Still there, still themed. Grab a coffee inside.
  - **Maison Collignon grocery** (56 Rue des Trois Frères) — Amélie's corner épicerie.
  - **Sacré-Cœur steps** — where she leads the blind man; and the base of the hill.
  - **The carousel** at the foot of the butte (near Place Saint-Pierre, below the basilica).
  - **Canal Saint-Martin** (your 16:45 stop) — where she skips stones across the water.
- **Moulin Rouge! (2001, Baz Luhrmann)** — the **red windmill cabaret** on Boulevard de Clichy (South Pigalle, right by your lunch zone). The real Moulin Rouge still lights up nightly.
- **La Vie en Rose / La Môme (2007)** — the **Édith Piaf** biopic; Piaf sang and grew up around these Montmartre/Pigalle streets. Hum "La Vie en Rose" on the basilica steps.
- **Ronin (1998)** — Robert De Niro thriller with chase scenes threading Paris.
- **Midnight in Paris (2011, Woody Allen)** — the whole nostalgic-Paris fantasy; the Montmartre/Left-Bank cafés and midnight streets are the film's soul.

### 🇮🇳 Bollywood in Paris
- **Befikre (2016)** — Ranveer Singh & Vaani Kapoor's Paris romance; they roam **Montmartre / Sacré-Cœur**, the Seine, and the Eiffel. Basically a Paris tourism reel.
- **Queen (2013/2014)** — Kangana Ranaut's Rani wanders **Montmartre and the Sacré-Cœur** area on her solo-in-Paris leg before Amsterdam — fitting, since that's literally your route too (Paris today → Amsterdam tomorrow).
