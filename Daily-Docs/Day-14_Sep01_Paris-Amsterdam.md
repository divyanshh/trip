**Step by step:**
1. **Have the QR/e-ticket ready in the Eurostar app** (plus a screenshot backup) *before* you reach the station — French station wifi is flaky at 6am.
2. **Arrive ~55–60 min early** — this Eurostar has airport-style **security + a boarding gate that closes ~10 min before departure**. Not a hop-on regional train.
3. Scan **QR at the ticket gate** → bag through security → departure lounge.
4. **🛂 NO passport control — Paris→Amsterdam is Schengen-INTERNAL** (that's a domestic-style border; passport control is only on the *London* Eurostar). Keep your passport handy anyway, but expect **no stamp, no queue**.
5. Watch the board for the **platform + boarding gate**, then board and find your seat (coaches numbered along the platform).
6. **Onboard:** weak free wifi, power socket at seat, luggage racks at coach ends + overhead — keep your trolley in sight. **No checked baggage — you carry everything.**
7. **Amsterdam Centraal 10:50** — step off, walk out the front of the grand 1889 station straight onto the water. No passport control on arrival either.

### Why these spots
- **Canal belt / Nine Streets** — the UNESCO Golden-Age canal ring is why Amsterdam looks like Amsterdam; Nine Streets packs the best independent shops and cafés into nine tiny cross-streets. Slow wandering beats any checklist.
- **Jordaan** — the city's most atmospheric neighbourhood: hidden almshouse courtyards (hofjes), galleries, locals-only cafés. Where you *feel* Amsterdam.
- **'t Smalle** — a genuine "brown café" (bruin café = wood-panelled pub) from the 1780s with a floating canal terrace.
- **Begijnhof 💎** — a serene walled 14th-century courtyard hidden in plain sight, with the oldest wooden house in the city. Free, tranquil, easy to walk past. A highlight.
- **Bloemenmarkt** — the only floating flower market in the world; quick look + tulip-bulb souvenirs (buy "certified for import" packs if bringing home).
- **Red Light District** — beyond the obvious, it's the oldest, most beautiful medieval core around the Oude Kerk, best as an evening canal-lit walk.

## 🚊 TRANSPORT — Sep 1

- **Eurostar** Paris Gare du Nord → **Amsterdam Centraal** (details above). You arrive right in the centre — no airport transfer.
- **Within Amsterdam:** trams + metro + buses — just **TAP a contactless card/phone (OVpay)** when you board *and again when you get off* (tap out or you're overcharged). No paper tickets needed. **Trams 2 and 12** run from Centraal toward the Museumplein/Vondelpark side. A single ride is **~€3.40** capped by OVpay.
- Amsterdam is **very walkable and bikeable** — most of today's sights are a compact walking loop; save the tram for the Foodhallen hop.
- ⚠️ **Watch the bike lanes** — cyclists have priority and move fast; don't stand in the red-paved lanes.
- **Apps:** Citymapper / Google Maps (offline) for transit.

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD — Sep 1 (Amsterdam = Europe's best veg city)

- **Foodhallen** (Bellamyplein 51) — indoor food hall: **veg dim sum / bao, halloumi & falafel plates, Dutch cheese, wood-fired veg pizza, fresh juices.** Buzzy, social, perfect arrival lunch.
- **The Vegan Indian** — 100% plant-based Indian: **dal makhani, chana masala, paneer-style tikka (vegan), biryani, naan/roti.** Comforting, familiar spices after a travel day.
- **De Bolhoed** (Prinsengracht 60–62) — beloved veg/vegan canal-side institution: **veggie burritos, curries, big salads, homemade cakes.**
- **Meatless District** — trendy fully plant-based: **jackfruit dishes, plant burgers, bowls, brunch plates.** (Good backup.)
- **Tip:** install **HappyCow** — Amsterdam has dozens of all-veg spots; you rarely need to ask "is this veg?"
