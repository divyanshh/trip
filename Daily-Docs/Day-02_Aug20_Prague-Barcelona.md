## 📍 What to do (afternoon + evening) + why it matters

### 🍽️ Teresa Carles (all-veg) — your first Barcelona meal · ~14:30
- **Why:** 100% vegetarian/vegan, iconic Barcelona spot — zero risk of hidden meat/fish, perfect for easing in after travel.
- Area: **Carrer de Jovellanos 2** (near Plaça de Catalunya / Universitat). ~15–20 min by metro (L1/L3) or taxi.
- **Dishes:** the *"Golden Toast"* / avocado toasts, fresh veg-fruit juices & smoothies, wild-mushroom risotto, veg lasagna, quinoa & superfood salads, homemade cakes. Everything is veg — order freely.

### 🏛️ MNAC (Museu Nacional d'Art de Catalunya) + rooftop · ~17:30
- **Why:** grand palace atop Montjuïc with Romanesque + Catalan Modernist (Gaudí-era) art, plus one of the best **free-to-reach rooftop terraces** over Plaça d'Espanya. Perfect low-energy jetlag activity — culture + view without much walking.
- **3-min walk** uphill from your hotel. Rooftop *mirador* accessible with museum ticket.

### 🌆 Arenas de Barcelona rooftop · ~19:00
- **Why:** a former bullring turned mall — its **rooftop promenade is FREE**, giving a 360° panorama of Plaça d'Espanya, the Venetian towers, and Montjuïc. Sunset-friendly (~20:30 in late Aug).
- Directly across Plaça d'Espanya from your hotel. Exterior panoramic lift (small fee) or interior escalators (free).

### 🍽️ Carrer de Blai — veg pintxos crawl (dinner, Poble Sec) · ~20:00
- **Why:** a pedestrian street of **pintxos bars** (Basque small bites on bread, ~€1–2 each). Grab what you want, pay by the toothpick count — fun, cheap, and lots of veg options.
- **Veg-safe picks:** *tortilla de patatas* (potato omelette — has egg), *patatas bravas*, *pan con tomate* (tomato bread), *pimientos de padrón* (grilled peppers), grilled veg, mushroom, cheese, olive/tomato pintxos.
- **Avoid:** anything topped with ham/anchovy/tuna. Ask *"¿Esto es vegetariano? ¿Sin jamón, sin atún?"*
- ~10 min walk from the hotel into Poble Sec.

### ⛲ Magic Fountain of Montjuïc — light + music + water show · ~21:30
- **Why:** Barcelona's signature **free** evening spectacle — choreographed water, colored light and music at the foot of MNAC. A gentle, iconic way to close a jetlag day.
- Summer sessions typically **Thu–Sat, ~21:30–22:30** — **confirm the day's schedule** (occasionally paused for drought). Right below MNAC, ~5 min from hotel.
