## 📍 Tour Stops — What & Why

### 🏰 Sagrada Família (exterior on tour)
Gaudí's unfinished UNESCO basilica — the icon of Barcelona. The tour covers the **Nativity & Passion facades**. Staggering even from outside; the interior (your evening ticket) is the real magic.

### 🌈 Park Güell
Gaudí's whimsical public park — the mosaic serpentine bench, gingerbread gatehouses, the tiled dragon (*el drac*), and a sweeping viewpoint over the city. WanderOn should cover the group entry to the Monumental Zone.

### 🏠 Casa Batlló + Passeig de Gràcia
Gaudí's "House of Bones" — bone-like balconies and a scaled roof, on Barcelona's grandest boulevard. **Casa Milà / La Pedrera** (also Gaudí) is nearby. Tour covers the exteriors.

### 🏛️ Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic)
The medieval heart of Barcelona — the **Barcelona Cathedral**, narrow stone lanes, hidden plazas (Plaça Reial, Plaça Sant Felip Neri), Roman ruins. Best on foot with the guide.

## 🍽️ Vegetarian Food

**Lunch (near the Gothic route):**
- **CatBar** — craft-beer bar with fully **vegan burgers**, in the Gothic/Born area. Perfect quick tour-day lunch. Try the *classic vegan burger* + fries.
- **Flax & Kale** — huge veg/vegan menu near El Raval / Plaça de Catalunya. *Poke-style bowls, veg burgers, kale dishes, smoothies.*

**Dinner:**
- **Teresa Carles** (100% vegetarian) — near Plaça de Catalunya, walkable from the Gothic Quarter. *Wild-mushroom risotto, veg lasagna, quinoa & superfood salads, fresh juices, homemade cakes.* Zero hidden-meat risk — order freely.

**Anywhere backup veg staples:**
*Pan con tomate* (tomato bread), *tortilla de patatas* (potato omelette — contains egg), *patatas bravas*, *pimientos de padrón*, *escalivada* (roasted veg), *crema catalana* dessert. Use the **HappyCow app** to find nearby veg spots on the fly.
⚠️ Avoid *paella*/rice unless confirmed veg (usually meat/fish stock) and anything with *jamón* or *atún*.

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Filmed Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)

Barcelona is one of cinema's favorite cities — you'll literally walk through movie sets today.

- 🎥 **Vicky Cristina Barcelona (2008, Woody Allen)** — the film that made the world fall for this city. Scarlett Johansson, Penélope Cruz and Javier Bardem move through **Park Güell** (you're there mid-morning — the mosaic terrace is straight out of the film), the **Gothic Quarter's** lamplit lanes, and multiple **Gaudí** landmarks. Keep an eye out at Park Güell and Barri Gòtic — you're standing in the frames.
- 🎬 **Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara (2011)** — the ultimate Bollywood road-trip film has **Barcelona segments** woven through it; the city's streets, plazas and energy are all over the movie. (Its scuba scene is on the Costa Brava — that's tomorrow, Day 04.)
- 🕯️ **Perfume: The Story of a Murderer (2006)** — Barcelona's old town and the medieval lanes of Catalonia doubled for 18th-century France. The claustrophobic Gothic alleys you'll walk this afternoon are exactly the mood.
- ⚔️ **Cathedral of the Sea (La Catedral del Mar)** — the hit historical drama centered on the **Santa Maria del Mar** basilica in the Born/Gothic district. If you pass it on the walking tour, that's the "cathedral of the sea" itself.
- 🎶 **"Barcelona" — Freddie Mercury & Montserrat Caballé (1988)** — the soaring operatic duet that became the anthem of the **1992 Barcelona Olympics**. Queen's Freddie Mercury and Catalan opera legend Montserrat Caballé — a rock god and a diva, the city's signature song. Play it tonight; it hits differently when you're actually here. (And yes — she's named after tomorrow's mountain, Montserrat.)
