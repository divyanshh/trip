## 🚊 TRANSPORT
- All three cities are on the main line **south of Amsterdam** — trains every ~10–15 min, so slippage is easy to absorb.
- **Tap OVpay** (contactless card/phone) in AND out at each station — no paper tickets needed. ~**€30–40** round trip for the day.
- Within Rotterdam: metro/tram, or just walk (sights cluster centrally); a **2-hour local ticket is €5**, day €11.

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)
- **Girl with a Pearl Earring (2003)** ⭐ — Scarlett Johansson & Colin Firth; **Johannes Vermeer's Delft** is the setting. Today is the perfect bookend: you walk **Vermeer's town** and stand at **his grave in the Oude Kerk**, then meet **the actual painting** at the Mauritshuis a few train-minutes later — plus his *View of Delft*, painted from the very canals you strolled.
- **The Hague** — the seat of international justice (Peace Palace / ICJ) features in countless political dramas and documentaries.
- **Rotterdam** — its ultra-modern skyline (Cube Houses, Erasmus Bridge, Markthal) is a favourite of architecture films, music videos and ad shoots; the city was rebuilt bold after WWII.
