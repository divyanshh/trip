## 🗺️ WHAT TO DO + WHY
Toledo is a UNESCO World Heritage hilltop city, the former capital of Spain, famous as the **"City of Three Cultures"** where Christian, Jewish, and Muslim communities coexisted for centuries — you can read it in the layered architecture street by street. The tour is guided and included.

- **🔴 Toledo Cathedral (Catedral Primada)** — one of the greatest High Gothic cathedrals in Europe (1226–1493). Don't miss the soaring nave, the baroque _Transparente_ skylight, El Greco's _The Disrobing of Christ (El Expolio)_ in the sacristy, and the treasury monstrance. The spiritual heart of Catholic Spain.
- **🔴 Alcázar of Toledo** — the massive stone fortress crowning the highest point of the city (today the Army Museum). Its four-tower silhouette defines the skyline — the definitive Toledo photo.
- **🔴 Panoramic viewpoint over the Río Tajo (Tagus)** — the classic postcard vista of the walled city wrapped by the river gorge, usually from the **Mirador del Valle** across the river (many tours stop the bus here). Camera ready.
- **🟡 Jewish Quarter (Judería)** — narrow lanes, the **Sinagoga del Tránsito** and **Santa María la Blanca** (a synagogue built in Moorish style). This is where the "three cultures" story is most tangible.
- **🟡 El Greco trail** — the painter lived and worked here; his masterpiece _The Burial of the Count of Orgaz_ hangs in the **Iglesia de Santo Tomé**, and the **Museo del Greco** sits in the old Jewish quarter. Toledo is inseparable from El Greco.
- **🟢 Wander + shopping** — the guide usually gives free time. Toledo is famous for **damascene metalwork** (gold-inlaid black steel) and **swords/knives** (Europe's premier bladesmithing city). Fine souvenirs — but if buying blades, they must be **checked-in on flights, never carry-on**.

## 🍬 VEGETARIAN FOOD — Toledo
- **🔴 Marzipan / Mazapán de Toledo** — the city's signature sweet, made only of **ground almonds + sugar, 100% vegetarian**. Buy from a historic obrador like **Santo Tomé** — the perfect edible souvenir and totally safe for you.
- Lunch is usually free time in town. Veg-safe Spanish plates: **tortilla española** (potato omelette — ⚠️ contains egg, skip if egg-free), **pan con tomate**, **patatas bravas** (confirm the sauce), **pisto manchego** (La Mancha's ratatouille — the classic local veg dish), **ensalada**, and **Manchego cheese** boards. Use the phrase above and confirm no _jamón_ (ham) or _atún_ (tuna).

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)
Toledo is above all **the city of El Greco** — the Greek-born master painter who made it his home; his brooding **_View of Toledo_** is one of the most famous landscapes in Western art (that exact skyline is what you'll photograph from the Mirador). Stand at the **Tagus viewpoint** and you're inside the painting.
- A **medieval walled city famous for Toledo steel** — its swordsmiths gave the world the finest blades for centuries, and "Toledo steel" echoes through fantasy sword lore (the Excalibur / _Lord of the Rings_ replica-blade tradition traces back to smiths here). Look for it in the **sword shops** during free time.
- Its perfectly preserved medieval streets, the **Alcázar**, and the **Cathedral** have served as a historic backdrop in many **Spanish period films and series**.
- **Cervantes / Don Quixote country** lies just south in La Mancha — the windmill-tilting spirit of Spain's greatest novel belongs to this region.
- **Bollywood filmed less here** than on the coast, but the **_Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara_ (ZNMD)** road-trip spirit — three friends, open Spanish highways, discovery — runs straight through central Spain, and your Madrid↔Toledo drive is exactly that landscape.
- 📍 Exact spots to feel it: **Mirador del Valle** (the _View of Toledo_ panorama), the **Alcázar** and **Toledo Cathedral** (period-film skyline), the **Museo del Greco** and **Iglesia de Santo Tomé** (El Greco's masterpieces), and the old-town **sword/damascene workshops**.
