## ☀️ THE DAY — Catamaran Boat Party (14:30–17:30) ✅ included

- **Nothing to book** — just confirm the meeting point with WanderOn and show up.

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD (name the spots)
Ibiza is very veg/vegan aware. The rule today: **eat solid meals BEFORE each drinking session** (before the boat, and again before Ushuaïa). Inside the club food is expensive and limited.

**Named veg-friendly spots near Playa d'en Bossa:**
- **Passion Café (Playa d'en Bossa)** — bowls, avocado toast, veggie curries, smoothies. Great breakfast/lunch fuel.
- **The Real Vegan Café (Playa d'en Bossa)** — fully vegan burgers, wraps, bowls.
- **Ca n'Alfredo (Ibiza Town)** — classic Ibicenco restaurant with solid veg rice/veg options if you want a proper sit-down.
- **Los Pasajeros (Ibiza Town)** — cheap veg pasta, salads, patatas.

**Meal-by-meal:**
- **Breakfast (~10:30):** Hotel buffet — eggs (if you take them), toast, fruit, yogurt, pastries, juice. Eat big.
- **Pre-boat lunch (most important):** something substantial — **pizza margherita, pasta napolitana/arrabbiata, veggie bocadillo (cheese + tomato baguette), patatas bravas, Spanish tortilla (potato omelette), salad, hummus.** Say **"sin carne, sin pescado."**
- **On the boat:** usually just drinks + light nibbles — **don't count on veg food there.** Carry a couple of snack bars.
- **Post-boat / pre-Ushuaïa snack (~18:00):** refuel — pizza slice, **falafel wrap**, fries, fruit. Get carbs in before drinking again.
- **Inside Ushuaïa:** drinks-focused; eat properly beforehand.
- 💧 **Hydration is food today** — carry a water bottle, alternate alcohol with water all day. This is what protects tomorrow's ferry + La Tomatina energy.

- **Two club nights in a row (SHM + Guetta) = manage your body.** Sleep in, eat 3 real meals, hydrate relentlessly. The people who "die" in Ibiza are the ones who skip food + water.
- 🍹 **Never leave your drink unattended**; don't accept open drinks from strangers; buy your own.

- 🧴 **SPF 50**, reapply after swimming, hat + sunglasses — a boat sunburn ruins the whole night.
