- Any Indian snacks you're carrying (thepla, namkeen, khakhra) — perfect ferry food
- **Onboard fallback:** fries, crisps, coffee, a plain veg sandwich if one exists — backup only.

### 🥘 Valencia veg dinner (light, near hotel)
- **⭐ Paella de verduras (vegetable paella)** — **Valencia is the BIRTHPLACE of paella.** The veg version is THE signature dish. ⚠️ Many paella bases use meat/fish stock — always ask for the **veg-only** version: _"sin carne, sin pescado, sin marisco, sin caldo de carne — con caldo vegetal."_
- Other easy veg: **patatas bravas, pan con tomate, Spanish tortilla** (potato omelette — confirm no meat), **croquetas de verduras, gazpacho** (cold, light), **horchata** (Valencia's famous tiger-nut drink — veg + local), **fartons.**
- Valencia is very veg-friendly — HappyCow lists dedicated spots near Exe Rey Don Jaime. **Keep it LIGHT tonight.**
