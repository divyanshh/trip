## 🚆 GETTING TO VERSAILLES (read this — the ticket is different)
- **Line: RER C** toward **"Versailles Château – Rive Gauche"** (this exact station — NOT Versailles-Chantiers, NOT Rive Droite). Rive Gauche is a flat ~10-min walk to the palace gates.
- From Gare du Nord: metro to an RER C stop (e.g. **Saint-Michel–Notre-Dame** or **Invalides**), then RER C outbound. Let your maps app pick the live fastest route.
- **⚠️ Ticket: Versailles is Zone 4.** Buy a dedicated **"Paris ↔ Versailles Château" RER C ticket (~€4.50 each way)** at the machine — a **standard t+ metro ticket does NOT reach Versailles** and you'll be fined. Keep the paper ticket to tap out at Versailles.
- Journey: **~40–45 min** each way.

## 🎫 TICKETS
- **Versailles — 10:00 ✅ booked — QR ready.** Scan at Entrance A. ~30-min grace. Download the QR offline the night before. Free audioguide included.
- **Louvre — 15:00 ✅ booked — QR ready.** **Enter via the CARROUSEL DU LOUVRE** underground entrance, NOT the main glass Pyramid — far shorter line. ~30-min grace. **Mona Lisa first**, then loop the icons on the way out.
- **Arc de Triomphe (~€16) — buy on-site day-of.** Not pre-booked, no need. Rooftop = the best night vantage for the Eiffel sparkle.
- 📥 Download **both** QR codes offline the night before.

## ⭐ WHAT TO SEE + WHY
- **Hall of Mirrors (Galerie des Glaces):** 73 m of mirrored arcades where the 1919 Treaty of Versailles was signed — the most iconic room in France. Go slow; best light mid-morning.
- **Mona Lisa:** smaller than you expect, behind glass, always mobbed — that's why you hit her first, snap it, move on.
- **Winged Victory of Samothrace:** on the Daru staircase — arguably more breathtaking than the Mona Lisa. Don't miss it.
- **Venus de Milo:** Sully wing, ground floor, Greek antiquities.
- **💎 Medieval Louvre / the moat:** basement (Sully) — the original 12th-c. fortress foundations. Quiet, atmospheric, most tourists skip it.

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD
- ⚠️ **L'As du Fallafel (Marais)** reopens after Shabbat, but it's off your route today — the Little India spots below are the plan.
- **🔴 Little India — Krishna Bhavan / Saravana Bhavan** (Rue du Faubourg Saint-Denis / Passage Brady, near your hostel line) — **pure-veg South Indian**: masala dosa, idli-vada-sambar, thali, uttapam, paneer curries, filter coffee. Familiar, filling fuel before the Louvre and again for dinner.
- Quick central alt near the Louvre: a **veg crêpe/galette** or a falafel wrap to save time.
- **Versailles note:** palace food is limited and pricey — carry a **snack + water bottle**, or eat well at breakfast and save the real meal for lunch back in Paris.
