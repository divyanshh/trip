**The three central sights (walkable loop):**
- **Gran Vía** — Madrid's grand boulevard, the "Spanish Broadway": theaters, neon, early-20th-century architecture. Best walked at dusk as it lights up.
- **Puerta del Sol** — the literal heart of Spain (Kilómetro Cero, where all Spanish roads are measured), the bear-and-strawberry-tree statue (_El Oso y el Madroño_), and the famous clock. Always alive.
- **Plaza Mayor** — the grand 17th-century arcaded square, uniform red facades, cafés under the arches, street performers. The classic Madrid postcard. (Just south: Mercado de San Miguel food hall.)

## 🍽️ VEGETARIAN FOOD — Madrid is easy for veg
- **⭐ Indian veg near Lavapiés** — Madrid's South-Asian quarter. Take the Metro toward **Lavapiés** for reliable pure-veg Indian: **thalis, dosas, paneer curries, dal** — great comfort food and the best pre-flamenco option.
- **Artemisa** — well-known **vegetarian tapas** spot near Sol: croquetas de verdura, veg paella, salads, grilled vegetables. A certain choice close to the sights.
- **Mercado de San Miguel** (by Plaza Mayor) — gourmet food hall for a light graze: **patatas bravas, pan con tomate, Spanish tortilla** (potato omelette), cheeses, olives. Confirm ingredients.
- Spanish dinner runs late — eating ~20:00 before a ~20:45 show works perfectly; or a fuller meal after (~22:00) if you'd rather.

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)
Madrid is the **capital of Pedro Almodóvar** — Spain's most celebrated director shot much of his work in and around these streets: **_Volver_**, **_Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown_**, and the Oscar-winning **_All About My Mother_**. As you walk **Gran Vía** and the old center, you're moving through his cinematic Madrid.
- **_Open Your Eyes / Abre los ojos_ (1997)** — the twisty Spanish thriller set in Madrid, later remade by Hollywood as **_Vanilla Sky_** (Tom Cruise).
- **_The Day of the Beast (El día de la Bestia)_** — the cult horror-comedy that famously climaxes on the **Schweppes tower on Gran Vía / Plaza de Callao** — glance up at that building as you pass.
- **Flamenco's spiritual home is Andalusia**, but Madrid's tablaos are where most travelers first see it live — tonight's show is that tradition up close.
- 📍 Exact spots to feel it: **Gran Vía** (Almodóvar + _Day of the Beast_ skyline), **Plaza Mayor** and **Puerta del Sol** (postcard Madrid seen in countless Spanish films).
