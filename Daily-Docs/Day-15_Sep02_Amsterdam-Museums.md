## 🎫 TICKETS

- 🎨 **Van Gogh Museum — ✅ booked, 09:00 (QR ready).** Arrive ~08:50, join the timed-entry queue, scan the QR at the door, enter within 9:00–9:30. Large bags go to the cloakroom.
- 🖼️ **Rijksmuseum — to book (do this ASAP).** Book a **timed slot around 14:30** at the official Rijksmuseum site. It sells out on busy days — book a day or two ahead, not at the door. QR → scan at entrance.
- 🔴 **Anne Frank House — to book (still sold out — keep refreshing).** Book **only on the official Anne Frank site** — any reseller is a scam. Tickets are timed-entry and release in batches. **Refresh several times a day, check every Amsterdam day and every slot** — cancellation drops appear unpredictably. If you get any slot, take it immediately. They're strict on time; no large bags; no photos inside the annex.
- ⭐ **Canal cruise — to book.** Book the small-boat guided cruise directly; small groups fill up. **Confirm the departure dock** so you know where to be at ~11:30.

## 🚊 TRANSPORT

- **Museumplein cluster:** Van Gogh (Museumplein 6) and Rijksmuseum (Museumstraat 1) are next to each other — 2-min walk. Vondelpark is 5 min further.
- **From hostel:** **Tram 2 or 12** toward Museumplein/Vondelpark. **Tap OVpay contactless on AND off** (tap out or you're overcharged). No paper ticket needed. Or walk ~20–25 min through the canals.

- ⚠️ **Watch the red-paved bike lanes** — cyclists have priority and move fast.

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD

- **Lunch — Foodhallen:** veg **bao, gyoza/dim sum, falafel & halloumi plates, wood-fired veg pizza, fresh juices.**
- **Dutch cheese note:** ⚠️ Many traditional Dutch cheeses (Gouda etc.) use **animal rennet** — not strictly vegetarian. Ask for a **vegetarian/vegetable-rennet** cheese if you want to be sure.
- **Stroopwafel:** ✅ vegetarian (caramel-syrup waffle) — safe and delicious for a snack.
- **Dinner — De Pijp:** **Vegan Junk Food Bar** (loaded fries, "chicken"-style burgers, all vegan), plus Middle-Eastern, Indian and Thai spots; the Albert Cuyp street-food area is nearby.
- **Also great any meal:** **De Bolhoed** (canal-side veg — burritos, curries, cakes), **The Vegan Indian** (dal makhani, chana masala, paneer-style tikka, biryani, naan), **Meatless District** (jackfruit dishes, plant burgers, bowls).
- **Tip:** Install **HappyCow** — Amsterdam is Europe's best veg city; you rarely need to ask "is this veg?" here.
- Handy phrase: **"Is dit vegetarisch? Geen vlees, geen vis."** (Is this vegetarian? No meat, no fish.)

### 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)

Amsterdam is a screen-legend city — you'll walk through scenes all day:

- ❤️ **The Fault in Our Stars (2014)** — the big one. Hazel and Gus visit the **Anne Frank House** (the interior stairwell + the "It's a metaphor" moment), and share the famous kiss on the **canal-side bench on Leidsegracht** (fans still find "their" bench — take the photo). They also stroll **Vondelpark** — where you'll be at ~16:45.
- 🇮🇳 **Queen (2014, Kangana Ranaut)** — *the* Bollywood Amsterdam moment. Rani's solo-traveller awakening plays out right here: her **hostel**, the **canals**, the **coffeeshop**, and a wander through the **Red Light District**. As a solo traveller yourself, this whole day is basically living Rani's Amsterdam chapter — lean into it.
- 🕴️ **Ocean's Twelve (2004)** — the heist crew works the Amsterdam canals and old-town lanes.
- 💎 **Diamonds Are Forever** — classic-era James Bond passes through the diamond city and its canals.
- 🔫 **The Hitman's Bodyguard (2017)** — the wild **canal boat / scooter chase** tears through these exact waterways and bridges — picture it from your cruise.
- 🎩 **Amsterdam (2022)** — the David O. Russell period drama that takes the city's name.
- 🖼️ **Girl with a Pearl Earring (2003)** — Vermeer's world; the actual painting lives in the **Mauritshuis** in The Hague (you can see it on your Sep 4 day trip). Vermeer's **The Milkmaid** is here in the **Rijksmuseum** — spot it today.
- 🎨 **The masters themselves:** the **Rijksmuseum** is where Rembrandt's colossal **Night Watch** hangs; the **Van Gogh Museum** is a whole film's worth of the man's life on the walls. Two of art history's biggest "characters," both on today's schedule.
