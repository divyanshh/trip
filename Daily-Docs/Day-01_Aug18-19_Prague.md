## 🗺️ Hour-by-hour timeline

> ⭐ **Priority key:** 🔴 must · 🟡 optional · ⚪ skip · 💎 hidden gem
> 🔴 **If jetlag hits, protect only these:** Old Town Square/Clock → Old Town Hall Tower → Castle + St. Vitus → Charles Bridge **day AND night.** Everything else is droppable.

### 09:00 · 🔴 Old Town Square + Astronomical Clock — *Free*
- Prague's medieval heart: pastel façades, the twin-spired **Týn Church**, and the **Astronomical Clock (Orloj, 1410)** — the world's oldest still-operating astronomical clock. On the hour the 12 Apostles parade past the windows.
- Heads-up: the hourly "show" is famously underwhelming — see it once, then go up the tower for the real payoff.

### 09:45 · 🔴 Old Town Hall Tower — *~350 CZK, walk-in*
- Climb (or lift) for **the single best rooftop panorama in Prague** — red roofs, spires, the castle across the river.
- This view is why you can safely **skip all the parks/hills** (Petřín, Letná) later. Buy at the entrance desk inside the Old Town Hall — no pre-booking.

### 10:15 · 🔴 Tram 22 up to the castle — *~40 CZK*
- **Tram 22** is *the* castle tram — it climbs the hill so you don't have to. Catch it toward **Pražský hrad** stop.
- Ticket: yellow machine at the stop or the PID Lítačka app; **validate on board** (stamp it) or you'll be fined. A 30-min ticket covers this ride.

### 10:45 · 🔴 Prague Castle + St. Vitus Cathedral + Golden Lane — *~450 CZK, walk-in (or hrad.cz)*
- The **largest ancient castle complex in the world** — seat of Bohemian kings, Holy Roman emperors, and today the Czech president.
- **St. Vitus Cathedral** (begun 1344): soaring Gothic, crown jewels, royal tombs.
- ⭐ **Don't miss:** the **Mucha stained-glass window** · the **noon guard change** (you'll be right there) · **Golden Lane** — tiny 16th-c. houses where **Kafka lived at No. 22**.
- Go now (morning): fewer crowds, and **St. Vitus closes ~16:00.** Buy the circuit ticket at the on-site Info Centres (2nd/3rd courtyards) — walk-in.

### 12:15 · 💎 Nový Svět (+ Strahov Library if quick) — *Free*
- **Nový Svět** ("New World"): a tourist-free cobbled **fairytale lane** just behind the castle — postcard Prague without the crowds.
- If time/energy allows, **Strahov Monastery Library** nearby has a jaw-dropping baroque hall. Best photos of the morning.

### 13:00 · 🔴 VEG LUNCH — *~350 CZK*
- 🌱 **Lehká Hlava ("Clear Head")** or **Maitrea** — both **100% vegetarian**, beautiful central interiors, ideal for a solo pure-veg traveler (same owners, both near Old Town).
- **Dishes:** at Lehká Hlava try the veg burger, quesadillas, or the daily veg risotto/curry; at Maitrea go for the "false meat" satay, veg noodles, or the tasting plates. Fresh juices at both.
- Back down: Tram 22 toward the river, or walk down through Malá Strana (~15 min, downhill) toward Charles Bridge.

### 14:15 · 🔴 Charles Bridge (daytime) + Lennon Wall + Kampa Island — *Free*
- **Charles Bridge** — commissioned by **Charles IV in 1357**, Gothic stone over the Vltava, lined with **30 baroque statues**, linking Old Town to Lesser Town. By day: buskers, artists, portrait painters.
- Right beside it: the **Lennon Wall** (ever-changing peace graffiti) and **Kampa Island** (leafy riverside). You'll cross the bridge again at night — a totally different experience.

### 15:00 · 🟡 Jewish Quarter (Josefov) — *walk free / ~500 CZK ticket*
- Europe's best-preserved former Jewish ghetto: the **Old-New Synagogue** (Europe's oldest active) and the haunting **Old Jewish Cemetery**.
- A combined ticket (~500 CZK) covers synagogues + cemetery; or just **walk the streets free** if you're low on energy.

### 16:00 · 💎 Speculum Alchemiae — *~200 CZK, walk-in*
- A **hidden medieval alchemy lab** with underground passages, near the Jewish Quarter — atmospheric guided peek into Emperor Rudolf II's alchemist Prague. Totally off the tourist track.

### 16:45 · 🟡 Wander Old Town / Lesser Town — *Free*
- Catch 💎 **Hanging Freud** (David Černý's dangling man over **Husova St** — right on your route) and 💎 **Klementinum** (stunning baroque library + astronomical tower, by Charles Bridge). Relax, coffee, soak in the streets.

### 17:45 · 💎 U Krále Brabantského — *drinks ~€6–10 / CZK equivalent*
- **Prague's oldest tavern (~1375)** — mead, candlelight, medieval vibe. Go **for the atmosphere and a drink** (kitchen isn't veg-friendly — save your appetite for dinner).

### 18:30 · 🔴 VEG DINNER — Dhaba Beas — *~400 CZK*
- 🌱 **Dhaba Beas** — Indian **vegetarian buffet** (pay by weight): dal, paneer sabzi, chana, rice, fresh rotis, salads — reliable comfort food after a long day.
- Backups if closed/full: **Country Life**, **Loving Hut** (both veg/vegan).

### 20:00 · 🔴 ⭐⭐ Charles Bridge at NIGHT — *Free*
- The bridge **empties out and lights up** — statues glow, the castle floodlit across the river. Completely different from the daytime crowd. **Best photos of the entire day.** Sunset is 20:13, so you catch blue hour → full dark right here.

### 21:15 · 💎 Nightcap (your call) — *your budget*
- **U Sudu** — labyrinth underground wine bar (chill, atmospheric, solo-friendly).
- **Cross Club** — steampunk multi-level club (Holešovice).
- **Karlovy Lázně** — 5-floor mega-club by the bridge.
- Other gems: **Anonymous Bar** (V-for-Vendetta speakeasy) · **Vzorkovna "Dog Bar"** (grungy maze) · **Black Angel's** (1930s speakeasy).

## ⚠️ Nightlife safety — red-light scam warning
- Prague has **no Amsterdam-style window district.** Its adult venues (strip / "gentlemen's" clubs) cluster around **Wenceslas Square / New Town** side streets (**Ve Smečkách, Perlová**) and the main station.
- These are **notorious for scams**: €500+ inflated bills, credit-card fraud, aggressive touts.
- **If you go:** never follow a tout, agree prices upfront, **cash only (never card)**, set a limit, leave if it feels off. Honestly — safer and more fun to stick to Charles Bridge at night, U Sudu, Cross Club, or Karlovy Lázně.
