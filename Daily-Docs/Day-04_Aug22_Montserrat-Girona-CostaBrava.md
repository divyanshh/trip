## 🏔️ Stop 1 — Montserrat
Catalonia's spiritual heart: a Benedictine monastery wedged into surreal pink-grey rock pinnacles at ~720m. The region's most iconic day trip, with unreal views.
- 🔴 **Santa Maria de Montserrat Monastery** — the basilica, free to enter.
- 🔴 **La Moreneta (the Black Madonna)** — the 12th-century black wooden Virgin, Catalonia's patron saint. Queue to walk up behind the altar and see/touch the orb she holds. The single must-do — go early, the line grows.
- 🔴 **Cable car (Aeri) or rack railway (Cremallera)** — the ride up/down the cliff face is spectacular. Confirm if your tour includes it or if it's a paid add-on (carry cash/card).
- 🟡 **Escolania boys' choir** — one of Europe's oldest, if timing lines up (don't bank on it).
- 🟡 **Sant Joan funicular / viewpoints** for panoramic photos if you get time.

**Veg here:** cafeteria + market stalls sell **local cheese, honey, bread, fresh fruit, nuts, and *mató* (fresh curd cheese)** — all veg-friendly. Otherwise lean on your packed snacks.

## 🐉 Stop 2 — Girona (your highlight)
Girona's perfectly preserved medieval old town (Barri Vell) doubled as **Braavos and King's Landing in Game of Thrones**. A compact, atmospheric, walkable maze — and the film-location star of the day.
- ⭐ **Girona Cathedral steps** — the grand staircase used as the **Great Sept of Baelor** in *Game of Thrones* Season 6 (Margaery & the High Sparrow scenes; Jaime rides up here). Climb them.
- ⭐ **Sant Martí church stairs / Pujada de Sant Domènec** — the "Braavos" streets where **Arya Stark is stabbed by the Waif** and runs bleeding through the crowd.
- 🔴 **Passeig de la Muralla** — walk the medieval city walls/ramparts for old-town + countryside views.
- 🔴 **Onyar River houses** — the postcard row of **colourful houses** reflected in the water; cross the **Eiffel Bridge (Pont de les Peixateries Velles)**, built by Gustave Eiffel's company.
- 🔴 **El Call (Jewish Quarter)** — one of Europe's best-preserved medieval Jewish quarters: narrow shadowy stone lanes and hidden courtyards.
- 💎 **Arab Baths (Banys Àrabs)** and the winding **Carrer de la Força** if time allows.
- 📸 **Lioness statue (Cul de la Lleona)** — legend says kissing her backside guarantees you'll return to Girona.

**Veg here:** Girona is foodie-friendly. Look for **pa amb tomàquet** (tomato bread), **patatas bravas**, **espinacas con garbanzos** (spinach + chickpeas), grilled veg, **crema catalana**. HappyCow → search Girona if the group lunch is light. (*Truita de patates* / potato omelette contains egg.)

## 🌊 Stop 3 — Costa Brava (Tossa de Mar)
The "wild coast" — pine-covered cliffs plunging into turquoise coves. Most tours stop at **Tossa de Mar**, the prettiest medieval-walled beach town on the coast.
- 🔴 **Vila Vella** — Tossa's 12th-century walled old town on the headland, with towers over the sea (the only fortified medieval town left on the Catalan coast).
- 🔴 **Platja Gran (main beach)** — golden crescent under the castle walls; dip your feet, walk the sand, photos.
- 🟡 Coastal path / lighthouse viewpoint for the classic cove shot.

## 🍽️ Vegetarian Food — Day Strategy
- **Snacks are your safety net** — use them, especially between Montserrat and lunch.
- **Group lunch:** tell the server the veg phrase immediately. Safe orders across Catalonia: *pa amb tomàquet, patatas bravas, ensalada (hold tuna/ham), pimientos de padrón, espinacas con garbanzos, gazpacho, pizza margherita / de verduras, pasta with tomato/veg, paella de verduras (ask if veg stock).*
- ⚠️ Watch-outs: paella/rice = fish/meat stock (ask); "vegetal" soups may have chicken base; "vegetable" tapas can hide *jamón* — say *"sin jamón."*
- **Dinner back in Barcelona (~20:30):** keep it light and quick — early day + flight tomorrow. **Teresa Carles** or **Flax & Kale** (all-veg, if open), a **Carrer de Blai** veg pintxos crawl, or simple pizza/pasta. Hydrate, then pack for Ibiza and sleep.

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Filmed Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)

Today is a genuine film-location pilgrimage — point these out on the ground:

- 🐉 **Game of Thrones (Season 6)** — **Girona's old town** is the day's headline set. The **Cathedral steps** = the **Great Sept of Baelor** (King's Landing); the **Jewish Quarter and Sant Martí / Pujada de Sant Domènec stairs** = the streets of **Braavos** where **Arya Stark runs bleeding from the Waif**. Walk the exact steps and lanes — they're barely changed from the show.
- 🌊 **Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara (2011)** — the famous **Costa Brava scuba-diving scene** was shot right here on this coast. It's the emotional core of the film: the **"Sí Señor"** deep-sea dive where Hrithik Roshan's character faces and conquers his fear of the water. As you stand at Tossa de Mar looking out at the Mediterranean, that's the sea from the movie. ⚠️ **No actual diving for you today** — you fly to Ibiza tomorrow and diving needs an 18–24h no-fly window. Surface swimming is fine; tanks are not.
- 🕯️ **Perfume: The Story of a Murderer (2006)** — **Girona** stood in for 18th-century France; its medieval lanes and the cathedral surrounds appear throughout. You're walking the film's streets.
- 🤖 **Westworld (HBO)** — **Girona** also features in the sci-fi series, its old stone architecture reworked into the show's world. Another reason the town feels cinematic in every direction.
- ⛰️ **Montserrat** — not a film set but the region's **sacred mountain monastery**, and the namesake of Catalan opera legend **Montserrat Caballé** (of the Freddie Mercury "Barcelona" duet from yesterday's doc). The mountain itself is the star.
