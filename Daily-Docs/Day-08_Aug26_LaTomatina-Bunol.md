The **_Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara_ finale was filmed right here in Buñol** — you are literally walking into that scene. Everything today bends around protecting this one hour.

---

## 🎒 WHAT TO WEAR / BRING (lay it out the NIGHT BEFORE)
**⚠️ THE THREE CRITICAL ITEMS — do not go without these:**
1. **Old CLOSED shoes you will THROW AWAY.** Not sandals, not flip-flops — the streets become an ankle-deep slurry of crushed tomato and you can get stepped on / lose footing. Closed-toe = protection. They'll be destroyed; bin them right after.
2. **Swim goggles.** ⚠️ Tomato juice is **acidic and genuinely stings the eyes.** Goggles are the single biggest comfort upgrade — they let you keep your eyes open and actually *enjoy* the fight.
3. **Waterproof phone pouch** (sealed, lanyard around neck). If you want ANY photos/video, this is the only way the phone survives. Assume everything gets soaked and pulped. **Better still: leave the phone on the bus/hotel and just live it.**

**Also wear/bring:**
- **Old T-shirt + old shorts you don't care about** (white shirt = classic look, but it WILL be stained red/orange permanently).
- A **fully sealed dry bag or ziplock** for anything that must stay dry (hotel key card, a little cash) — or better, **leave valuables locked at the hotel / on the coach.**
- **Minimal on your person.** No jewelry, no watch, no loose items — pockets get emptied by the chaos.
- **Sunscreen** before you leave (you're outdoors from ~09:00). Optional: small water bottle, cheap plastic poncho (most people skip it).

## 🍅 THE RULES OF THE FIGHT (official — follow them)
1. **⚠️ SQUASH / CRUSH tomatoes before throwing** — throw them soft, never as a hard whole projectile. The #1 safety rule.
2. **Only tomatoes** — no bottles, no hard objects.
3. **Do NOT rip anyone's clothes.**
4. **Stop the moment the second cannon fires.**
5. **Make way for the tomato trucks** — don't get pinned under them.

**Play it smart:** position **mid-crowd, not dead-center under the trucks** — plenty of action without the crush. **Keep goggles ON the whole hour.** Don't over-film — a couple of bursts, then put the phone away and actually be in it. Stay loosely near the WanderOn group / know the rally point (phones dead + soaked in a 20,000-person mob).

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD
**Veg phrase:** _"Soy vegetariano estricto, sin carne ni pescado ni marisco."_ Use **HappyCow** if you want a certain spot.
- **Breakfast: eat BIG at the hotel** — this is your real fuel; there's no good veg option in the Buñol crush.

- **Valencia dinner (easy, near hotel):** Valencia is the **home of paella** — order **⭐ _paella de verduras_ (vegetable paella)** and confirm the **veg-only** version: _"sin carne, sin pescado, sin marisco, sin caldo de carne — con caldo vegetal"_ (many paella bases use meat/fish stock — ask for veg stock). Other safe bets: **patatas bravas, pan con tomate, Spanish tortilla** (potato omelette — confirm no meat), **grilled veg, agua de Valencia** (the local drink), **horchata + fartons.**

### 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)

**🍅 BUÑOL / LA TOMATINA — THE BIG ONE (a bucket-list Bollywood pilgrimage):**
- **_Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara_ (2011)** filmed its **iconic La Tomatina climax right here in Buñol.** Hrithik Roshan, Farhan Akhtar, Abhay Deol (and Katrina Kaif) run into the tomato-fight finale — the euphoric, paint-it-red ending of the film that made an entire generation of Indians add La Tomatina to their bucket list.
- The scene plays out to **_"Ik Junoon (Paint It Red)"_** — the anthem of the fight. Hum it while you're in it.
- **📍 Say it out loud: you are literally standing inside that scene.** This is the Bollywood pilgrimage moment of the whole trip — the tomatoes flying around you are the exact chaos on screen. Have that in your head at 11:00 when the cannon fires. 🎥🍅

**🎥 VALENCIA (back tonight):**

- 📍 **Exact spot:** along the old Turia riverbed, ~15 min from the center. Too wrecked tonight — but it's the photo stop if you get a free hour tomorrow morning before Madrid.
