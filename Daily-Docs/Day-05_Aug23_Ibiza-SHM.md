## 🎧 THE MAIN EVENT — Swedish House Mafia @ Ushuaïa ✅ booked — QR ready
**Why it's special:** Ushuaïa is Ibiza's legendary **open-air poolside superclub** (attached to the Ushuaïa Hotel on Playa d'en Bossa). The stage sits over the pool, under the sky — a sunset-into-night spectacle. **Swedish House Mafia** (Axwell / Ingrosso / Angello) are one of the biggest house/EDM acts alive: expect anthems, wall-of-sound production, pyro, and 15,000 people going off at once.

**Using your ticket:**
- 🎫 **QR ticket — ✅ booked, QR ready.** Save it **offline** (screenshot + wallet app) before you fly. Max screen brightness at the gate.
- 🪪 **Carry a government photo ID** (passport/card). Ushuaïa can check at entry — keep it in a zipped pocket.
- 🚪 **Doors ~5 PM — get there early.** Early entry = closer to the stage/pool, shorter security line, and you catch the sunset build-up.
- 🎶 **Main set ~9:30–11 PM** — the peak. Position yourself before 9:00.
- 👕 **Dress up a bit** — Ushuaïa leans stylish (no sportswear/flip-flops for some areas). Cool, comfortable clothes; you're on your feet for hours in the heat.

### 🎵 Tracks you might hear live tonight
Swedish House Mafia catalogue: **"Don't You Worry Child," "Save the World," "Greyhound," "One (Your Name)," "Miami 2 Ibiza," "Leave the World Behind," "Heaven Takes You Home," "Moth to a Flame," "Lifetime."** Members' solo/side hits also creep in: Axwell Λ Ingrosso's **"Something New," "More Than You Know," "Sun Is Shining."** When any of these drop poolside at sunset, that's the moment.

- **Airport → hotel:** WanderOn/group transfer. If solo, an official airport taxi to Playa d'en Bossa runs **~€20–30** (10–15 min).

## 🥗 VEGETARIAN FOOD (name the spots)
🔴 **Eat a proper meal ~16:00 before the show** — food inside Ushuaïa is expensive and limited, and you'll be drinking + dancing for hours. Ibiza is genuinely veg/vegan-aware (it's a wellness island), so you're in good hands.

**Named veg-friendly spots to try:**
- **Passion Café (Playa d'en Bossa branch)** — all-day healthy spot: buddha bowls, hummus platters, avocado toast, veggie curries, smoothies. Reliable pre-show fuel close to the hotel.
- **The Real Vegan Café (Playa d'en Bossa)** — fully vegan: veggie burgers, wraps, bowls.
- **Wild Beets (Santa Gertrudis)** — dedicated plant-based, if you have a free day earlier.
- **Asubergine (Santa Gertrudis)** — farm-to-table, lots of veg, garden setting.
- **Los Pasajeros (Ibiza Town)** — cheap, cheerful; veg pasta, patatas, salads.

**Safe dishes to order anywhere:** margherita / verduras pizza, pasta pomodoro or arrabbiata, hummus + pita, falafel bowl, patatas bravas, Greek salad, veggie paella (**ask for veg stock — "sin caldo de carne ni pescado"**), veggie burger. ⚠️ *Ensalada payesa* often hides fish — say **"sin pescado."**

- 💧 **Hydrate hard** — carry water, drink between alcoholic drinks. August + open-air club = easy dehydration.
- 🍫 Keep **granola bars / nuts** in your pocket for a mid-night energy top-up.

## 🛟 CLUB-NIGHT PRACTICALS + SOLO SAFETY
- 🍹 **Watch your drink at all times** — never leave it unattended, don't accept opened drinks from strangers, buy your own at the bar. This is the #1 club rule.
- 👥 **If you're with the crew, agree a meeting point** in case phones die or you get separated, plus a shared plan to get back to the hotel. Solo, tell one person roughly when you plan to leave.
- 🔋 **Charge phone to 100% + carry a power bank** — you need it for the QR, photos, maps, and finding people.
- 🪪 **Carry ID + one card + some cash** in a zipped/crossbody pocket. Minimal valuables — pickpockets work crowded clubs. Leave your passport in the hotel safe if the QR/entry accepts a card ID.
- 🚕 **Plan the ride home before you're tired** — know the walk route, or which rank to queue at. Never wander back alone heavily drunk; if solo, keep the hotel name on your phone lock screen or a card.
- 🧴 **Sunscreen before the show** (open-air, evening sun still bites) + optional **earplugs** to protect hearing over a long night.
- 💊 Ibiza clubs have **zero tolerance for anything illegal** — as a foreign traveler, don't risk it.
- 😴 **Don't burn out** — the big group Guetta night + boat party are tomorrow. Enjoy tonight but keep some gas.

## 🎬 Movies, Songs & Pop Culture Here (Bollywood + Hollywood)
Ibiza is the **EDM capital of the world** and the spiritual home of nearly every party anthem you'll hear this week:
- **"Ibiza" (2018, Netflix)** — rom-com literally built around chasing a DJ across the island's clubs.
- **"It's All Gone Pete Tong" (2004)** — cult mockumentary about a hedonistic Ibiza DJ who goes deaf; "Pete Tong" is Cockney rhyming slang for "wrong."
- **"Kevin & Perry Go Large" (2000)** — Brit comedy where two teens chase their dream of DJing in Ibiza; peak cheesy-clubbing lore.
- **"The White Island"** — Ibiza's nickname (*Isla Blanca*), also the title of Ibiza party documentaries.
- **Club residency legend:** David Guetta built the island's superclub era with his **"F*** Me I'm Famous"** parties; **Swedish House Mafia** (who you see tonight) and their sunset Ushuaïa shows are modern folklore. **Ushuaïa, Hï Ibiza, Pacha, Amnesia** and DC-10 are the temples — each with decades of anthem history.
- 📌 **Bollywood note:** Ibiza itself is rarely a filming location, but the *sound* is everywhere in Indian party culture — the SHM/Guetta big-room house you'll hear tonight is the DNA behind countless Bollywood club remixes and festival sets. Tonight you're hearing it at the source.
